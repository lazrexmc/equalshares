The Roll Call

Fund proxy votes, per category, with receipts.

Funds that hold voting shares vote them on your behalf: on directors, executive pay, climate reports, mergers, at the company meetings of every stock they hold. Once a year the SEC requires each fund to file the full list of those votes (Form N-PX). It is public, and almost nobody can read it. This page is one such filing made readable: one fund, one proxy year, every vote lot, grouped by the SEC's own categories, with a receipt to the filing for every row.

How to read this page

Terms first; the numbers below are computed from the filing and every one of them can be opened as a list. Four strangers read an earlier version of this page and could not tell what a row was, so:

Fund
The fund named in the line above the page is one series of a larger registrant (the trust that files with the SEC). The filing covers that one series and one proxy year.
Proxy year
Form N-PX covers votes cast from July 1 to June 30. The period shown is the year's end date.
Proposal
One item on one company's ballot: a director election, a pay plan, a shareholder's climate resolution. The fund's vote on it is what a reader usually wants.
Record, or vote lot
One row of this page. The filing reports a proposal in one or more blocks, and each block holds one or more lots: a block of shares voted one way, with its own share count. One fund can vote different lots of the same proposal differently; that is the filing's own structure, shown as filed, and it is why a proposal can appear several times. Each row says which lot of how many it is.
Category
The SEC's own category, as the fund filed it. This page does not classify anything. A record the fund tagged with several categories is counted under the first one.
Proposed by
The filing's voteSource: ISSUER means the company's own management put the item forward; SECURITY HOLDER means a shareholder did.
How voted
FOR, AGAINST, ABSTAIN, or WITHHOLD as filed. WITHHOLD is the director-election way of not supporting a nominee where AGAINST is not on the ballot. Anything else the filing put in this field (say-on-pay frequency answers like "ONE YEAR" or "1.0") is shown verbatim and marked unparseable; it is never guessed into one of the four.
% FOR
Of the lots with a readable vote in that cell, the share voted FOR, with the count beside it. It is split by who proposed the item because "FOR" means support for management on a management item and support for a shareholder on a shareholder item. It says nothing about what any board wanted.
Mgmt rec (as filed)
The filing's managementRecommendation field, reproduced verbatim on each lot. It is a per-lot field, and this page checks whether it behaves like a board's recommendation before resting anything on it; the result of that check is stated in "Where these numbers come from". No headline on this page is computed from it.
Unparseable / absent in source
Two different states, never merged: unparseable means the filing gave a value this page does not understand (shown raw); absent in source means the filing gave no value at all, for example a proposal filed with zero lots (a withdrawn resolution).
Zero-share lots
A lot reported with 0 shares is the fund stating that a voting block held no shares at that meeting. Shown as filed.
Receipts
Form N-PX has no per-row anchor, so there is one receipt per filing: EDGAR's rendered vote table, stored at ingest time, never constructed. Each row tells you where to look in it: the row's position in the filing, its proposal number, and the issuer, CUSIP and meeting date to search for.
EqualShares
An independent project that makes fund proxy votes readable. Not affiliated with any fund, registrant, or the SEC. Every number here is computed from the filing by a fingerprinted run; none are typed.
